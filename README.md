## Featured Project

### German Electricity Market Analysis — Solar PV & Day-Ahead Prices

I recently completed a Python and Jupyter Notebook project analyzing day-ahead electricity prices in the German-Luxembourg bidding zone using ENTSO-E data.

The project focuses on how solar PV generation affects intraday price patterns, negative price hours, and the market value captured by solar assets.

**Key topics covered:**

* ENTSO-E data collection with Python
* Day-ahead price and solar generation analysis
* Merit order effect
* Negative price hours
* Solar capture rates
* Solar cannibalisation
* 2021–2022 energy crisis impact on price formation

**Main finding:**
Solar generation is increasingly linked to lower daytime electricity prices after the crisis period, and solar capture rates weakened significantly by 2025.

**Repository:**
[German Electricity Market Analysis — Solar PV & Day-Ahead Prices](https://github.com/andreas-walliser/energy-market-price-analysis)
